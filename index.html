Projects

Hands-on design, prototyping, and mechanical testing.

IMU-Based Smart Rehabilitation Sleeve

A wearable rehabilitation concept using an IMU on the forearm to track elbow movement during bicep curls.

The concept is intended to cue the patient to transition between flexion and extension at a desired angle.

Anatomically Accurate Head Model

Designed a 3D model of the brain’s ventricular system in Fusion 360 to support an anatomically representative brain phantom for traumatic brain injury research.

Used CAD and additive manufacturing to prototype a six-part mold, with attention to design accuracy, dimensional precision, and manufacturability.

Variable Length Orthopedic Screw

Designed and prototyped a variable length orthopedic screw in SolidWorks to reduce inventory requirements for clinical settings.

Validated device performance through cantilever and axial pullout strength testing throughout the design process.
